Privacy Policy – Deep Bridge Test

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Meta developer application Deep Bridge Test and related messaging API testing (including WhatsApp Business Platform and similar integrations).

Who we are

Deep Bridge Test is an internal testing application operated for development and integration purposes. Contact: deepbridgetest@gmail.com.

Data we may process

  • Phone numbers used for test messaging
  • Message content you send or receive through the WhatsApp Business Platform (or similar APIs) during testing
  • Technical logs needed to verify webhooks and API delivery (for example timestamps and message IDs)

How we use data

We use this data only to send and receive test messages, debug integrations, and confirm that callbacks/webhooks work. We do not sell personal data. We do not use test message content for advertising.

Sharing

Message delivery requires sharing necessary data with Meta / WhatsApp (and other providers you connect) as part of their platform services. We do not share test data with unrelated third parties for marketing.

Retention

Test logs and messages may be kept only as long as needed for development and then deleted when testing ends or upon request.

Your choices and deletion

To request deletion of test data associated with this app, email deepbridgetest@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion – Deep Bridge Test”. We will delete retained test data within a reasonable time.

Children

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for users under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy as the test app evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
